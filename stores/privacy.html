Veilforge privacy policy

Effective 19 August 2026. Veilforge is a single-player idle RPG.

What we collect

The game stores progress on your device (browser localStorage, or the equivalent WebView store). That save can include skill XP, inventory, combat state, and settings. It does not include your name, email, or payment details — we do not run an account system in version 1.0.

What we do not collect

No analytics SDK, advertising identifier, crash reporter, or third-party tracker ships in 1.0. The web, Android, iOS, and Steam builds use fonts already on the device. Progress never leaves your device unless you export a save yourself.

Export

You may export or import a save file yourself. If you share that file, whoever you send it to can read your progress.

Children

The game is a fantasy combat idle title. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Support for this build is through the project's source repository issues. There is no account system and we do not collect email.
