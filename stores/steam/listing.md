# Veilforge — The Last Workshop

**Short description (80 chars)**  
Commit to a duskbound idle craft — or Halt, and pay the tax.

**About this game**

The last workshop at the edge of the Veil still takes apprentices. Twenty-two skills, a 120 cap, and a bank that will not hold the forest. You do not cancel a job because the pile is full — you keep the XP, and you feel the Halt.

Veilforge is a deep idle / incremental RPG: gather, smith, cook, chart the dusk, keep a drove, and walk the combat triangle with weapon specials that actually change a fight. Offline progress resolves when you return. Saves stay on your machine (export / import anytime).

**Features**

- 22 skills with mastery, guild ranks, and pool checkpoints
- 400+ items and actions, 120 uniquely named monsters, dungeons and bounties
- Halt-and-switch: changing jobs is a decision, not a misclick
- Auto-eat, vows, spells, and a combat triangle with specials
- Unique crafts: Course pillars, Whisper heat, Soil plots, Drove pens, Chart slots
- Keyboard-and-mouse desktop UI; pause when the window is hidden
